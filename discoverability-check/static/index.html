About Discotherm

Discotherm is an artist discoverability diagnostic — it measures how easily a new listener could stumble onto an artist and connect with their music. It is not a popularity tracker: a huge streaming count doesn't guarantee an artist is easy to discover, and a small one doesn't mean they're invisible.

It runs five diagnostics:

  • Catalogue DepthShare of tracks with full lyric metadata (lyrics + synced lyrics) that makes them visible to lyric-driven discovery.
  • Stream ConcentrationWhether listening is spread across the catalogue or piled onto just one or two tracks.
  • Playlist ReachHow far the music extends across editorial and algorithmic playlists.
  • Mood CoherenceHow consistent the emotional signature of the lyrics is across the catalogue.
  • Platform GapWhere the artist is present — or missing — across streaming and social platforms.

Powered by two data sources. Musixmatch supplies catalogue search, catalogue metadata, rich-sync coverage, and lyric mood analysis. Songstats supplies streaming numbers, playlist placements, and cross-platform stats.

Discoverability matters because being hard to find is a measurable harm — great music that no one can reach is a problem you can actually diagnose and fix.
